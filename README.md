# Borders-are-Lava

THE BORDERS ARE LAVA

This arcade game includes a ball and two players, who instead of being opponents, play cooperatively with each other in order to overcome the obstacles of the game and prevent the ball from touching the borders of the screen. It is not just about preventing the ball from touching the borders, but the players also have to manage their individual health - affected by various factors - and collect perks to last in the game longer and achieve a higher score.

Objective: - Keep the ball in play by hitting with the player racket. A life is lost if the ball touches the border. - Maximize score by hitting the ball as many times as possible. - Keep the players alive by ensuring their health remains more than 0. - Avoid restricted zones. - Collect player perks for spped-up, health-up, and extra lives. - Collect defense perks for the different types of balls that may change randomly.

Player Controls: - Red player: A, W, S, D - Blue player: Arrow keys

Lives: - Game starts with 3 lives. - A life is lost every time the ball hits the border. - Game ends when the lives become 0.

Health: - Health bars are exclusive to players. - A total of 100 health bars per player. - Decreases by 5 bars when a special ball is hit without its defined defense perk. - Decreases by 5 bars per second for staying in a restricted zone. - If the health of a player becomes 0, the player dies. /The game continues as a single player game.

Player perks: - Speed increase: Increases the speed of the player that collected the perk. - Health increase: Restores 20 health points. - Invincibility: Prevents life loss when ball hits borders in the time that the perk is valid.

Ball types: - Standard ball - Spikey ball - Poison ball - Electric ball - Fire ball - Ice ball

Defense perks: - Standard racket (for standard ball) - Sword (for spikey ball) - Poison resistant racket (for poison ball) - Rubber slipper (for electric ball) - Water gun (for fire ball) - Ice hockey stick (for ice ball)

Restricted areas: - Spawned randomly for 10 - 20 seconds. - Exclusive to player. - If a player stays in their restricted area, their health decreases by 5 bars per second.

Modes: - Player can select the mode they want to play the game in at the start of the game. - Dark mode - Light mod
